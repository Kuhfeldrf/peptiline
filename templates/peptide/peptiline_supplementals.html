Figures

S1 Fig. Interactive rendering of the sequence heatmap in Fig 2A

Interactive version of the sequence heatmap of β-casein peptides from bitter samples shown as Fig 2A in the main text. The line trace shows averaged peptide abundance and the strip below shows peptide-count density by sequence position; hover over any point for exact values.

S2 Fig. Stacked bar plot of functional peptide distribution

Distribution of 21 functional categories across bitter and non-bitter samples with scaled bar heights representing total summed abundance.

S3 Fig. Stacked bar plot of protein-origin distribution by abundance

Contribution of protein sources (Beta-casein, Alpha-S1-casein, Alpha-S2-casein, Kappa-casein, Other Proteins) to total peptide abundance in bitter and non-bitter samples.

S4 Fig. Peptigram comparison heatmap of β-casein bitter peptides

Comparison figure showing Peptigram's visualization of β-casein variants A1 and A2 peptides, demonstrating mapping differences with PeptiLine's approach.

S5 Fig. Compact heatmap of β-casein A1/A2 bitter peptides

PeptiLine's own compact, sequence-position-binned heatmap of β-casein A1 and A2 bitter peptides, shown for direct comparison with Peptigram's rendering of the same case study (S4 Fig).
