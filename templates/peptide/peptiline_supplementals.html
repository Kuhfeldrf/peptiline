S1 Fig. Scatter plot matrix of peptide-abundance correlations between bitterness groups

Contribution of protein sources (Beta-casein, Alpha-S1-casein, Alpha-S2-casein, Kappa-casein, Other Proteins) to total peptide abundance in bitter and non-bitter samples.

Total unique peptide counts across bitterness groups with error bars representing replicate variability.

S5 Fig. Total peptide abundance by bitterness group

Summed peptide abundances across bitterness groups with error bars representing replicate variability.

S6 Fig. Peptigram comparison heatmap of β-casein bitter peptides

Comparison figure showing Peptigram's visualization of β-casein variants A1 and A2 peptides, demonstrating mapping differences with PeptiLine's approach.
