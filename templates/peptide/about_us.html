About PeptiLine

PeptiLine is a Django web application with three linked dashboards: Data Transformation, Data Analysis, and Heatmap Visualization.

This standalone deployment does not yet include live MBPDB search -- upload a pre-downloaded MBPDB TSV, or your own functional annotation table, in the Data Transformation module instead.
