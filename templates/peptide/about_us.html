About PeptiLine

PeptiLine is a Django web application with three linked dashboards: Data Transformation, Data Analysis, and Heatmap Visualization.

Data Transformation includes a live MBPDB search (BLAST- and exact-match against the Milk Bioactive Peptide Database) for annotating peptidomic results with known bioactive functions -- uploading a pre-downloaded MBPDB TSV, or your own functional annotation table, is also supported for those who prefer it.
